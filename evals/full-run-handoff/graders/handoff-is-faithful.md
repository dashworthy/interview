The interview must end with a handoff document that faithfully records what happened.

PASS if the written document captures the actual interview: the questions that were asked with the chosen answers (a Q&A record, not just a vague prose summary), the decisions reached, any blindspots surfaced, and concrete next steps.

FAIL if no document is written, or the document is a generic essay about vegetable gardening that does not reflect the specific questions asked and answers chosen during this interview.
